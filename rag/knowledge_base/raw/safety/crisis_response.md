# Crisis Response Policy

## Immediate crisis routing
<!-- chunk_id: crisis_immediate_routing -->
If someone may hurt themselves or another person, has taken an overdose, is in immediate danger, is experiencing abuse, or cannot stay safe, stop normal coping guidance and prioritize immediate human help. In the United States, call or text 988 or use the 988 Lifeline chat. Call emergency services when danger is immediate.

## Contact a trusted person
<!-- chunk_id: crisis_trusted_person -->
Encourage the person to contact someone they trust and, when possible, stay near another person while help is arranged. They may move away from immediate means of harm only if they can do so safely. The chatbot must not try to manage a crisis alone.

## Do not promise secrecy
<!-- chunk_id: crisis_no_secrecy -->
Do not promise secrecy when someone may hurt themselves or another person. Respond calmly without debating, shaming, judging, or minimizing. Explain that immediate safety and contact with crisis or emergency support take priority.
