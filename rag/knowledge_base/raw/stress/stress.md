# Stress Management

## Break a problem into steps
<!-- chunk_id: stress_break_into_steps -->
When many demands feel overwhelming, make the problem smaller. Choose one manageable next action, take a short pause or gentle movement break if possible, and ask a trusted person for support when needed.

## Prioritize what matters now
<!-- chunk_id: stress_prioritize_tasks -->
Write down what is truly urgent today, what can wait until later, and what is outside your control. Focus on one item rather than trying to solve everything at once. Limit stressful news or social media when it adds to the overload.
