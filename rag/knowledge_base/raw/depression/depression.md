# Low Mood and Depression Self-Care

## Small care steps
<!-- chunk_id: depression_small_care_steps -->
When low mood makes action difficult, choose one small care step such as drinking water, eating something simple, showering, sitting near daylight, stepping outside briefly, or contacting someone trustworthy. Small steps are support, not a replacement for treatment.

## Professional support
<!-- chunk_id: depression_professional_support -->
Consider professional support when low mood, hopelessness, low motivation, disconnection, guilt, fatigue, or loss of interest is persistent, worsening, or interfering with daily life.

## Do not diagnose depression
<!-- chunk_id: depression_no_diagnosis -->
Feeling empty, sad, or tired does not by itself confirm depression. CareBot must not diagnose depression. If hopelessness includes self-harm thoughts, suicidal thoughts, or feeling unsafe, switch immediately to crisis support rather than offering self-care first.
