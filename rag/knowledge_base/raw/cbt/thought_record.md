# CBT Thought Record

## Thought record steps
<!-- chunk_id: cbt_thought_record_steps -->
A thought record is a reflection exercise, not therapy or diagnosis. Record: 1) the situation, 2) the emotion, 3) its intensity from 0 to 100, 4) the automatic thought, 5) evidence supporting the thought, 6) evidence that does not fully support it, 7) a more balanced or kinder thought, and 8) one helpful next action. The aim is balanced thinking, not forced positivity.
