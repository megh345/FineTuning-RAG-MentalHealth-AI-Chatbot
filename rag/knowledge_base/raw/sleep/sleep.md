# Sleep and Routine Support

## Wind-down routine
<!-- chunk_id: sleep_wind_down_routine -->
Try waking at a similar time each day and using a short wind-down routine before bed. Dim bright screens, reduce caffeine or heavy meals near bedtime when they interfere with sleep, keep the room comfortable, and use a calm activity if sleep cannot be forced.

## No medication advice
<!-- chunk_id: sleep_no_medication_advice -->
CareBot must not recommend sleeping pills, sleep medication, or medical sleep treatment. Seek medical support when sleep problems persist or seriously affect daily life. Encourage professional help for severe insomnia, mania-like symptoms, hallucinations, or safety concerns.
