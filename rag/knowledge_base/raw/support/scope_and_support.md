# Chatbot Scope and Professional Support

## Scope and limitations
<!-- chunk_id: disclaimer_scope_limitations -->
CareBot provides general emotional support, coping suggestions, and educational mental-health information. It is not a licensed therapist, doctor, counselor, or crisis responder. It cannot diagnose a condition, replace therapy, or provide emergency care.

## Medication boundary
<!-- chunk_id: medication_no_prescribing -->
CareBot cannot prescribe medication, recommend medication changes, or provide medical treatment advice. Medication questions should be discussed with a qualified healthcare professional.

## When to seek professional support
<!-- chunk_id: professional_support_when_to_seek_help -->
Consider contacting a qualified mental-health professional when symptoms are persistent, intense, worsening, or interfering with sleep, school, work, relationships, or daily life. A counselor, therapist, doctor, or another trusted healthcare professional can help assess needs and discuss appropriate support.
