# Breathing Exercises for Stress and Anxiety

## Slow counted breathing
<!-- chunk_id: breathing_slow_count_5 -->
Sit, stand, or lie in a comfortable position and loosen tight clothing. Breathe in gently through the nose and out gently through the mouth. If comfortable, count slowly from 1 to 5 while breathing in and again while breathing out. Continue for a few minutes without forcing the breath; a shorter count is fine.

## Optional support, not a cure
<!-- chunk_id: breathing_not_a_cure -->
Breathing is an optional calming exercise, not a cure for anxiety or panic, and it does not need to be done perfectly. Seek urgent help for chest pain, fainting, severe breathing difficulty, immediate danger, or thoughts of self-harm.
