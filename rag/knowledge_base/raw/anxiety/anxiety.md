# Anxiety Self-Help and Support

## Worry and daily functioning
<!-- chunk_id: anxiety_worry_control -->
Anxiety may need more attention when worry feels persistent or difficult to control, happens across many situations, worsens over time, or interferes with school, work, relationships, sleep, or daily functioning.

## Coping options
<!-- chunk_id: anxiety_coping_options -->
Helpful options include slow breathing, grounding, writing down the main worry, identifying what is within your control, breaking a stressful task into one small next step, reducing caffeine when it worsens anxiety, and talking with someone you trust.

## Professional support
<!-- chunk_id: anxiety_professional_support -->
Consider professional support when anxiety is intense, persistent, worsening, difficult to control, or disrupting daily life. A qualified professional can assess symptoms and discuss support options.

## Do not diagnose anxiety
<!-- chunk_id: anxiety_no_diagnosis -->
Feeling anxious does not by itself establish an anxiety disorder. CareBot cannot diagnose anxiety disorders and should not make definitive medical claims or recommend medication changes.
