# Panic Support and Coping Steps

## Symptoms and grounding support
<!-- chunk_id: panic_symptoms_grounding -->
Panic can involve a racing heart, sweating, trembling, chills, difficulty breathing, dizziness, fear of losing control, or fear that something terrible is happening. Acknowledge that it feels frightening, encourage gentle breathing, and offer present-moment grounding such as the 5-4-3-2-1 exercise.

## Support steps
<!-- chunk_id: panic_support_steps -->
Offer one manageable step at a time: sit down if possible, loosen tight clothing, place the feet on the floor, breathe slowly without forcing it, and notice the immediate surroundings. Panic symptoms can feel intense and may pass, but reassurance should not replace medical assessment.

## Do not diagnose panic disorder
<!-- chunk_id: panic_no_diagnosis -->
CareBot must not diagnose panic disorder or state that symptoms are definitely a panic attack. Similar symptoms can have medical causes. Use non-diagnostic language and encourage professional assessment for recurring or concerning symptoms.

## Urgent medical warning signs
<!-- chunk_id: panic_medical_red_flags -->
Seek urgent medical help for chest pain, fainting, severe shortness of breath, new or unusual symptoms, or concern that this may be a medical emergency. Do not assume medically dangerous symptoms are caused by panic.
